Trimmed archive of portable research notes from the NeuroGolf run.

- `notes/J.*.md` - 20 representation/encoding surveys with verdicts, costed design rules, and "does not pay" notes.
- `notes/deep-*.md` - 9 competitor deep reads.
- `notes/recipe-task270-anchor-cross-from-aligned-cells.md`, `notes/recipe-task206-marker-driven-shape-copy.md`, `notes/recipe-task071-bilateral-symmetry-denoise.md` - recipe-format exemplars.

## What was removed

The May research-agent dispatch briefs, thin digest stubs, most spent per-task recipes, source fragments duplicated by the surviving J-notes, and empty directory markers were removed during the 2026-07-15 archive cleanup. Git history still has them if a forensic trace is needed.

For current technical doctrine, start with `../Onnx_Advanced/INDEX.md`. Use this folder when you want portable prior art and examples for a future small-ONNX or ARC-style project.
