# Deep-read: `neurogolf-multi-source-onnx-solver.ipynb`

Source: `Research/primary_sources/neurogolf-multi-source-onnx-solver.ipynb` (177 KB, 14 code cells, 4,654 lines). The "engine" referenced by `neurogolf-2026-rule-based-onnx-solver.ipynb`'s blender. Pulls task ONNXs from 13 source notebooks, runs `solve_task()` per task with a ~30-detector / ~30-builder pattern library plus 1/2/3-layer learned-Conv fallbacks, then blends + overrides + dim-scrubs into a final 400-task `submission.zip`. Validation uses `nu.check_network` + `nu.score_network` and `_static_ok` (size, banned-op, static-shape filter). Cell 0–1 = data + utils. Cells 3–5 = ~30 ONNX builders. Cells 6–7 = ~30 detectors. Cells 8–10 = Gather builders + spatial solvers + analysis. Cell 11 = `solve_task()` orchestration. Cells 12–13 = per-task solve loop + 8-step blend pipeline.

The pattern library overlaps ours on color-perm / transpose / rot180 / rot90 / rot270 / slice-extract / constant-output, but is significantly broader on **geometric** (translation, tile, scale up/down, mirror-concat, self-concat, quad-mirror, frame-translation, period-row/col-tile, bbox-then-tile, crop-then-scale, anti-transpose, frame-border, row/col-broadcast, border-add, fixed-row-select, dilate-3×3) and **content-dependent** (bbox-fill, hole-fill, outline-extract, keep-largest/smallest-color, crop-plus-color, h/v/diag/both-symmetry-completion). It also has two general spatial-permutation solvers (zero-ambiguity and relaxed-ambiguity) that infer per-pixel Gather indices from train pairs, plus PyTorch-trained Conv1×1/Conv3×3/two-layer/three-layer fallbacks for tasks no rule matches. Cost-saving tricks of note: `Slice + Pad` content-only operations (avoids the 36k full-canvas memory tax); Constant nodes instead of initializers (opset 17, ir_version 8 — diverges from our pinned opset 10/ir 10); `_compact_mask` collapses identical-channel masks to single channel; spatial-then-channel-Gather composites solve "rotate + recolor" in one graph.

## Ranked `[Alpha]`-tagged candidates from this notebook

These are pattern ideas not yet in our library. Each names the detector + builder source and a rough cost estimate (ours, opset-10 stack — multi-source-onnx-solver runs opset 17 so its raw costs may not transfer). Estimated ARC hit count is rough — hand-mine before committing to any one of them.

### Tier S — high leverage, low effort (start here)

1. **`[Alpha]` Translation (frame shift)** — output = input shifted by (dr, dc), zero-padded outside. Detector: `detect_translation` (cell 6) brute-forces `(dr, dc) ∈ [-10, 10]²` and checks shape-preserved equality. Builder: `make_cheap_framed_translation` (cell 3) — Slice + Pad. Cost: ~36k memory + ~50 params (Slice/Pad initializers) → ~14–15 pts. Estimated hits: 5–15 tasks.
2. **`[Alpha]` Color-replace (general, multi-mapping)** — generalizes our color-perm to multiple {src→dst} mappings without requiring all 10 colors observed. Detector: `detect_color_replace` (cell 6). Builder: `make_conv1x1_onnx(build_color_map_weight(cmap))` (cells 3 + 8) — 1×1 Conv with permutation weight `(C, C, 1, 1)`. Cost: ~90k → ~13.6 pts. Estimated hits: 5–15 (some overlap with our 13). May replace `_detect_color_perm` once and-for-all; add `require_all_colors=False` mode.
3. **`[Alpha]` Color-swap (2-color)** — swap colors A↔B. Detector: `detect_color_swap` (cell 6) — exactly one swap pair found; verify direction observed in both directions. Builder: channel Gather with two indices swapped — same as our color-perm machinery, just with a 2-element permutation. Cost: ~10 → ~22.7 pts. Estimated hits: 2–6.
4. **`[Alpha]` Self-concat-h / self-concat-v** — output = `[input | input]` horizontally or vertically. Detectors: `detect_self_concat_h` / `detect_self_concat_v` (cell 6). Builders: `make_cheap_self_concat_w` / `make_cheap_self_concat_h` (cell 3) — Slice + Concat. Cost: ~36k memory + small initializers → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 2–5.
5. **`[Alpha]` Mirror-h-concat / mirror-v-concat** — output = `[input | flip(input)]`. Detectors: `detect_mirror_h_concat` / `detect_mirror_v_concat` (cell 6). Builders: `make_cheap_concat_flip_w` / `make_cheap_concat_flip_h` (cell 3) — Slice + Slice-reverse + Concat. Cost: ~36k → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 4–10.

### Tier A — medium leverage, low–medium effort

6. **`[Alpha]` Cheap-rot180-framed** (replaces our `fixed_rot180_network` for solved tasks 87, 140) — Slice content + reverse + Pad to canvas instead of full-canvas Gather×2. Builder: `make_cheap_rot180_framed` (cell 3). Cost reduction on existing solved tasks: free pts via lower cost. Verify it's actually cheaper under our opset-10 stack first (the notebook uses opset 17 + Constant nodes). Low effort.
7. **`[Alpha]` Anti-transpose** — output = transpose then rot180 (= flip over anti-diagonal). Detector: `detect_anti_transpose` (cell 6). Builder: `make_cheap_anti_transpose_framed` (cell 3) — Slice + Transpose + Slice-reverse + Pad. Cost: ~36k → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 1–3.
8. **`[Alpha]` Tile** — output = `np.tile(input, (tr, tc))` (full input, integer multiples). Detector: `detect_tile` (cell 6). Builder: `make_cheap_tile` (cell 3) — Slice + Tile. Cost: ~50k → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 3–8.
9. **`[Alpha]` Pixel-permutation (general)** — most powerful single primitive in the notebook. For tasks with same input/output shape, infers a per-pixel Gather index by matching pixel signatures across train pairs. Detector: `detect_pixel_permutation` (cell 6). Builder: `make_gather_onnx` (cell 3) — Reshape + Gather + Reshape (1D length-900 Gather table). Cost: ~3,600 (params for `int64[900]` table) + ~36k memory → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 10–25 (subsumes many specific spatial-only patterns including some of ours).
10. **`[Alpha]` Spatial-zero-ambiguity solver** — generalization of pixel-permutation that handles arbitrary same-shape input/output. Detector: `try_spatial_zero_ambiguity` (cell 8) — for each output pixel, find input pixels with matching cross-pair signatures; if exactly one, take it. Builder: same as #9. Cost: same. Estimated hits: 5–15 above the strict pixel-permutation case.
11. **`[Alpha]` Period-row-tile / period-col-tile** — input has period P rows/cols; output tiles to larger height/width. Detectors: `detect_period_row_tiling` / `detect_period_col_tiling` (cell 6). Builders: `make_period_row_tile_onnx` / `make_period_col_tile_onnx` (cell 5) — Slice + Tile + Pad. Cost: ~50k → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 3–8.
12. **`[Alpha]` Frame-border** — overlay a 1-pixel border of fixed color over the input. Detector: `detect_frame_border` (cell 6). Builder: `make_frame_border_onnx` (cell 5) — Mul (interior keep mask) + Add (border color mask). Cost: ~36k memory + ~9k MACs (Mul+Add) + ~9k params (two `[1,C,30,30]` masks) → ~12.5 pts. Estimated hits: 2–5.
13. **`[Alpha]` Row-broadcast / col-broadcast** — output = repeat one specific row/col of input. Detectors: `detect_row_broadcast` / `detect_col_broadcast` (cell 6). Builders: `make_cheap_row_broadcast` / `make_cheap_col_broadcast` (cell 3) — Slice (1 row/col) + Tile + Pad. Cost: ~50k → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 1–4.
14. **`[Alpha]` Color-filter** — set specific colors to background (zero); keep others unchanged. Detector: `detect_color_filter` (cell 6). Builder: channel Gather mapping filtered colors to channel 0. Cost: ~10 → ~22.7 pts. Estimated hits: 3–8.
15. **`[Alpha]` Nonzero-recolor** — every nonzero cell becomes a single fixed color. Detector: `detect_nonzero_recolor` (cell 6). Builder: `make_conv1x1_onnx(build_nonzero_recolor_weight(tc))` — 1×1 Conv with all-channels→tc rows. Cost: ~90k → ~13.6 pts. Estimated hits: 3–8 (some overlap with our color-perm on tasks where input has only one nonzero color).
16. **`[Alpha]` BG-recolor** — every zero cell becomes a single fixed color. Detector: `detect_bg_recolor` (cell 6). Builder: `make_conv1x1_onnx(build_bg_recolor_weight(tc))`. Cost: ~90k → ~13.6 pts. Estimated hits: 1–3.
17. **`[Alpha]` Border-add** — pad input by `b` pixels on each side with `fill_color`. Detector: `detect_border_add` (cell 6). Builder: `make_cheap_border_add` (cell 3) — Slice (content) + Pad (border) + Add (fill mask). Cost: ~36k + ~9k → ~13 pts. Estimated hits: 2–5.

### Tier B — content-dependent, medium–high effort

18. **`[Alpha]` Quad-mirror** — output = 4 quadrants `[input, flip_h(input); flip_v(input), rot180(input)]`. Detector: `detect_quad_mirror` (cell 6). Builder: `make_cheap_quadrant_mirror` (cell 3) — multi-Slice/reverse + multi-Concat. Cost: ~70k → ~13.5 pts. Estimated hits: 1–3.
19. **`[Alpha]` Concat-rot180-w** — output = `[input | rot180(input)]` horizontally. Detector: `detect_concat_rot180_w` (cell 6). Builder: `make_cheap_concat_rot180_w` (cell 3). Estimated hits: 1–3.
20. **`[Alpha]` Crop-plus-color** — fixed-position crop + per-cell color map. Detector: `detect_crop_plus_color` (cell 6). Builder: `make_cheap_crop_then_color` (cell 3) — Slice + Gather (channel) + optional Mul (kill-mask) + Pad. Cost: ~36k → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 3–8.
21. **`[Alpha]` Bbox-fill** — fill the tight bounding-box of all non-zero cells with a fixed color. Detector: `detect_bbox_fill` (cell 6). Builder: `make_bbox_fill_onnx` (cell 4) — `Slice(channels 1..C) → ReduceMax(axis=1) → ReduceMax(axis=2) ⊗ ReduceMax(axis=3) → Mul(bg_ch) → Conv1×1(±1 weight) → Add(input)`. Cost: ~50k–80k → ~12.5 pts. Estimated hits: 3–8.
22. **`[Alpha]` Hole-fill** — flood-fill enclosed regions with target color. Detector: `detect_hole_fill` (cell 6) — BFS from border zeros, flag interior zeros. Builder: `make_hole_fill_onnx` (cell 4) — 14 iterations of `MaxPool 3×3 + Mul(bg)` to compute "outside-zero", then `Sub(bg_ch, outside) → interior_mask → Conv1×1 → Add(input)`. Cost: ~14 × 36k memory ≈ 500k + Conv ~90k → ~11 pts. Heavy but well-defined. Estimated hits: 3–8.
23. **`[Alpha]` H/V/diag/both-symmetry-completion** — fill zero cells with the reflected non-zero value across an axis. Detectors: `detect_h_symmetry_complete` / `detect_v_symmetry_complete` / `detect_diag_symmetry_complete` / `detect_both_symmetry_complete` (cell 6). Builder: `make_symmetry_completion_onnx` (cell 3) + appropriate `build_*_sym_complete_gathers` (cell 8) — multi-Gather of `inp_chw` with reflected indices, then `Max` over the gathered tensors. Cost per axis: ~3,600 (one Gather table) + ~36k memory → ~14 pts. Two-axis (h+v): ~7,200 + ~70k → ~13 pts. Estimated hits: 5–15.
24. **`[Alpha]` Outline-extract** — keep only the boundary cells of each connected non-zero region. Detector: `detect_outline_extract` (cell 6) — exhaustive grid scan checking 8-neighborhood. Builder: `make_outline_onnx` (cell 4) — `ReduceMax(channels 1..C) → Sub(1, ·) → Pad → MaxPool 3×3 → Mul(any_nonbg) → Sub(...) → 1×1 Conv(bg add)`. Cost: ~80k–120k → ~12 pts. Estimated hits: 1–3.
25. **`[Alpha]` Keep-largest-color / keep-smallest-color** — keep only cells of the most/least-common nonzero color. Detectors: `detect_keep_largest_color` / `detect_keep_smallest_color` (cell 6). Builders: `make_keep_largest_color_onnx` / `make_keep_smallest_color_onnx` (cell 4) — `ReduceSum(axes=2,3) → Equal → Cast → Mul`. Cost: ~50k–80k → ~12.5 pts. Estimated hits: 1–4.
26. **`[Alpha]` Dilate-3×3** — per-channel `MaxPool 3×3` (each color expands into its 8-neighborhood). Detector: `detect_dilate_3x3` (cell 7). Builder: `make_dilate_3x3_onnx` (cell 7). Cost: ~36k memory + small → ~14 pts. Estimated hits: 1–3.
27. **`[Alpha]` Bbox-then-tile** — tight bbox of nonzero, then tile to fill output. Detector: `detect_bbox_then_tile` (cell 6). Builder: `make_bbox_tile_onnx` (cell 5). Estimated hits: 1–3.
28. **`[Alpha]` Crop-then-scale** — fixed-position crop + nearest-neighbor upscale. Detector: `detect_crop_then_scale` (cell 6). Builder: `make_crop_scale_onnx` (cell 5) — Slice + Resize + Pad. Cost: depends on Resize accounting (verify under onnx-tool 1.0.1). Estimated hits: 1–3.

## Cost-side observations not yet integrated

- **`Slice + Pad` content-only operations** are systematically cheaper than full-canvas Gathers in the notebook's pattern library. Worth a re-cost check on our `fixed_rot180_network`, `fixed_flip_*_network`, `fixed_shift_*_network` to see if Slice+reverse+Pad would beat them. Risk: opset 10 may lack the `Slice` step argument (added opset 10 — confirm) and our pinned `Pad` op may have a different signature.
- **Constant nodes (opset 17) vs initializers (opset 10)** — the notebook constructs all weights as `Constant` op outputs (`onnx_tool.from_array(...)` then `make_node('Constant', [], [name], value=...)`) instead of initializers. Per `Research/A2_cost_metric_exploits.md`, `Constant`-vs-initializer can affect cost accounting; under our pinned opset 10 the difference may not matter or may favor initializers. Verify before adopting.
- **`_compact_mask` channel-collapse trick** — when a mask is identical across all 10 channels, replace `[1,C,1,1]` with `[1,1,1,1]` and let broadcast handle it. Saves `(C-1) × (HW or 1)` params on every masked op. Free win, easy to add to our existing builders.
