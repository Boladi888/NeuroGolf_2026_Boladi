## Old cost analysis (LIVE-structure era, superseded by the paint-over floor proof)

Big cost drivers measured by `tools/_score_v2` (sum of intermediate tensor sizes × elem-size,
excluding model input/output):

- 50 × `[1, 1, 30, 30]` F16 intermediates = 50 × 1800 = 90,000 bytes
- 72 × `[30]` F16 intermediates = 72 × 60 = 4,320 bytes
- 2 × `[1, 10, 30, 30]` F16 (post-Cast input + pre-Cast output) = 2 × 18,000 = 36,000 bytes
- 1 × `[1, 9, 30, 30]` F16 (Concat input) ≈ 16,200 bytes (Concat-output excluded as model output if dtype matches)
