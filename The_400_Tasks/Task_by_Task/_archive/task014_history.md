## Cost analysis (v1→v4 era, superseded by the v19 floor proof in task014.md)

Original live diagnostic cost: 45690 (memory=45598, params=92). It cast the full input to F16 and did channel-preserving gathers, keeping large [1,10,H,W] tensors around even after crop18.

Staged v4 static cost: 5270 (memory=5171, params=99). Full `profile_real_cost --arcgen 266` credited cost: 5437 (memory=5338, params=99). Dominant profiled tensors:
- `color_f32` [1,1,25,25] F32 = 2500B from the dilated Conv color decode.
- `canvas_dyn_u8` [1,1,30,30] UINT8 = 900B from padding the dynamic color-index crop before the final graph-output `Equal`.
- `color_u8` [1,1,25,25] UINT8 = 625B and `picked_mask_b` [1,1,25,25] BOOL = 625B for bbox discovery.
- `crop_dyn_u8` [1,1,h,w] UINT8 profiles to 168B on the full bundled arc-gen set; `pads_to_30` INT64 [8] = 64B.

Under-5k route note: the working route was not to remove the padded verifier contract, but to make a large tensor the final graph output. Uncapped exact dynamic Slice scored 4363 and passed visible/synthetic, but failed full A/B by 6 corpus cases because the live parent caps large/tie bboxes to the historical 18-cell crop window. v3 made the final `Pad` output but profiled over team-best on the full arc-gen set due to `crop_onehot_b` = 1680B; v4 pads the single-channel crop first and leaves the one-hot `Equal` as output, restoring a full-arcgen profile below 6099. (The v8→v18→v19 line then abandoned the color-decode entirely for the single-channel einsum-marginal crop.)
