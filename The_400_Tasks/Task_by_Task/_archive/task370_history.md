My decoded understanding of the "diagonal zero-mask projection" rule:
The grid has a dominant nonzero background, one marker cell of another color, and zero-valued holes. Find the farthest zero hole on any marker diagonal, use the vector from that hole to the marker as the projection step, repeatedly translate the full zero-hole mask by that step, and paint only original background cells with the marker color. Preserve original zero holes and the marker.
