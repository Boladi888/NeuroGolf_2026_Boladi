## Why it's dead
The cheap shortcut groups scene dots **by color**; but colors repeat across boxes (~20% of pairs here), so a color group can span 2–3 boxes. Splitting them requires **spatial clustering of NON-connected dots** (a box's `idx+1` dots are random pixels inside a ≤7×7 rect, not 8-connected) or **per-box correlation of the library dot-pattern against the scene** with cardinality-ordered used-mask consumption (process 3-dot → 2-dot → 1-dot, marking claimed dots used so a low-count box can't steal a high-count box's marker). That is exactly the irreducible matching combinatorics the structural line (v1→v198) and Sho's `corner_list_rect_solver` already pay.

Byte-bucket of Sho's live 26903 (`worker_bo_cld_bucket_bytes_20260709.py`): render+decode floor ≈ 5.3k (label30 F32 [1,1,30,30]=3600, canvas U8 [1,1,30,30]×2=1800, scene/is_bg half-planes), and ≈ 21k is the matching machinery — 30× `[1,1,15,17]` half-planes (7,650B) + `[1,255]` used/present flats (~2.8k) + `[1,6,11]`/`[1,6]`/`[1,11]` corner-list tensors (~2.5k) + small-tensor enumeration overhead. **The derivation, not the render, is the entire cost.** ORT has no Loop/recursion, so the ordered claim-and-consume must unroll into per-candidate static planes.
